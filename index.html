What is perplexity?

The short version, for your presentation.

🎲

Effective choices

A perplexity of 8 means the model was as unsure as if it were picking between 8 equally likely tokens at each step.
📉

Lower is better

Fluent, predictable text gets low perplexity. Random or broken text gets very high perplexity.
📏

Averaged per token

We take the geometric mean of the inverse probabilities, so long and short texts can be compared fairly.
🧪

Used to evaluate models

Researchers measure perplexity on held-out text to compare language models. A better model gives real text a lower score.

Reading the number

1Perfect certainty. The model knew every token. 2 to 20Confident. Typical for common phrases and facts. 20 to 200Normal for everyday, varied writing. 200 to 1000Surprised. Unusual wording or odd word order. 1000+Lost. Gibberish, typos or a different language.

Three equivalent formulas

Product form: PPL = ( Π 1/Pi )1/N
Log form: PPL = exp( −1/N Σ ln Pi )
Bits form: PPL = 2H, where H is the average surprise in bits per token

Good to know

Why does the first token get a probability?

GPT-2 has no start-of-text token in your input, so we add a special end-of-text token in front. That lets the model predict your very first token like any other.

Why are tokens not always whole words?

The tokenizer splits text into pieces like " Paris", "ing" or "Python". Rare words get cut into several tokens, and each piece is scored separately.

Can I compare perplexity across different models?

Not directly. Different models use different tokenizers, so N and the probabilities are not on the same scale. Compare scores from the same model only.

Does low perplexity mean the text is true?

No. It only means the text is predictable. "The capital of France is Berlin" can still score low if the model finds the phrasing familiar. Perplexity measures fluency, not facts.
